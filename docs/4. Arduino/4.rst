2.4 Add Library
---------------

What are Libraries ?

Libraries are a collection of code that makes it easy for you to connect 
to a sensor,display, module, etc.  

There are hundreds of additional libraries available on the Internet for download.

2.5 Project
-----------

|image5|

Alright, let’s get straight to our projects. In this kit, there are 14 sensors 
and modules. We will make you know the smart home deeply from the simple sensor.

However, if you are professional with Arduino. You can skip theses steps and 
assemble the smart home kit directly(**there is assembly video in the folder**).

**Note: In this course, the interface of each sensor / module marked with(G,-, GND) 
indicates the negative pole, G is connected to G, - or GND of sensor shield or control 
board; “V” is positive pole and connected with V, VCC or 5V.**
